Punpun Onodera is an ordinary elementary-school kid. He smiles, laughs, likes to play and look at the stars, like a regular kid.

Punpun's home is dysfunctional. His father is violent, his mother is emotionally unstable and neglectful. One day, Punpun's father violently assaults his mother, leaving her seriously injured and hospitalized. Following her Hospitalization, he abandons the family.

Punpun finds solace in stars, space, and his school crush Aiko. Actually, he also has an eccentric uncle, Yuichi Onodera, and his imaginary friend "God", but I will come back to them later. 

Punpun is head over heels for Aiko. Aiko herself, is strange, intense and unhappy. Her mother belongs to a cultish religious organization, and Aiko desperately wants to escape her home. She tells Punpun about Kagoshima, where she once lived, and the two make a childish promise that they'll go there together.

Punpun hypes himself up to do it, escaping his life to go with the love of his life. He psyches himself out to the point where he feels an overwhelming sense of urgency and responsibility of it, and he ends up not going with Aiko. 

Punpun is naturally a bit of a wimp.
