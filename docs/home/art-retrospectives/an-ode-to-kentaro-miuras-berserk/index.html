My favourite piece of art of all time, and I feel blessed and grateful to have a genius like Kentaro Miura present a part of his soul to us. I don't want to talk about Berserk, read it yourself, if you haven't already. I have instead, written my own short non-canon story that takes place in the Berserk Universe, and runs independent to the plot of Berserk.

Rest in Peace Miura. 

Emperor Ganishka

Daiba

Warning: Topics include War, War Crimes, Psychopathy and PTSD.


"The Kushan dynasty never saw a reason to get involved with The Midland and Tudor war, we faced troubles with some skirmishes within our ranks, as is usual, but firmly sit on all the wealth of the Sikkimese, Mauryas, and the Cholas." 

a brief silence enters the room, as the King's councilmember continues

"Still, It's in Kushan's best interest if Midland lose the war, we are close neighbours, and rumor has it that the Midland Army have an ace up their sleeve, a white hawk. "

King Ganishka, the ever ambitious Emperor of Kushan listened not to the bickering of his Councilmen, because he had already been planning a Midland Invasion for the past 6 months. 

You can't be too careful in the den of viper called The Royal Palace, but only a handful of Ganishka's most loyal servants knew of the plot. His most loyal of them was Daiba.

He met a sorcerer during his Sikkim conquest named Daiba. He could fly and manipulate water and wind, and was well versed in the Occult.

During his Sikkim expedition, Ganishka came across an odd rock, Crimson red and what looked like features of a visage, a shallow beat could be felt within the rock, it was obvious this rock was of the occult. 

Shortly after encountering the rock, Ganishka comes across Daiba, who joined his ranks 2 days later as his Court Sorcerer. Daiba Examines the rock and proclaims it as "The Egg of the King", a fated rock that reveals itself only under pure suffering.

"A king's trophy, for the egg contains a lake of blood, and in blood only will the egg hatch."

Daiba's Prophesy taps into Ganishka's clear bloodlust. The Councilmen did not trust Daiba, for he felt like an outsider whispering dark crude sorcery to the kings ears, only titillating the sanguinarian king further. But Kushan wouldn't go to war for the next two years.

The Sikkim expedition was an expensive endeavor. Kushan lost 1/5th of its infantry, and The Kingdom was fatigued. Despite this Kushan still had over 350,000 troops, along with the artillery, mobilized spanning to the borders of Paneria. 

(For reference, at the apex of the 100 year war, Tudor had 150,000 mobilized men and Midland were 80,000 small. )

But, timing of the war made no sense for kushan to get involved. Midland might be small, but they've been stubborn on the battlefield, and whispers of the hawks reputation and excellence on the battlefield reached the King's ears more than a few times.

"We'll clean up the mess when the time comes"

Emperor Ganishka, as he dismisses his court.
